# Value Metrics Catalog

Each example class defines a small set of measurable indicators. Use these when describing an example's **Value & Effect**.

## Order Operations

| KPI | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Time-to-resolve | Elapsed time from exception trigger to evidenced closure. |
| Gate compliance | Share of cases where every required human gate was passed with evidence. |
| Evidence completeness | Share of required evidence artifacts present at closure. |
| Audit reconstructability | Number of products whose artifacts, in combination, reconstruct the outcome. |

## Procurement

| KPI | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Approval accuracy | Share of purchase actions executed only after required approvals. |
| Cycle time | Time from request to receipt for standard categories. |
| Segregation compliance | Share of requests where requester did not approve their own request. |
| Exception closure | Share of exceptions closed with a recorded reason. |

## Talent Acquisition

| KPI | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Human-decision integrity | Share of selection and offer decisions made by accountable humans. |
| Evidence completeness | Share of hires with requisition, criteria, feedback, and approval evidence. |
| Cycle time | Time from requisition to offer for a role family. |
| Automation scope | Share of automated steps limited to administration and evidence organization. |

## Customer Service

| KPI | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| First-resolution accuracy | Share of cases resolved without escalation after evidence review. |
| Consent compliance | Share of customer-impacting actions with recorded consent or approval. |
| Communication completeness | Share of cases with acknowledgement and closure status recorded. |
| Case reconstructability | Number of product artifacts reconstructing the case. |

## Predictive Maintenance

| KPI | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Signal-to-intervention | Share of risk signals that reach an approved intervention. |
| Gate compliance | Share of interventions with required safety or cost review. |
| Evidence quality | Share of interventions closed with CMMS and notification evidence. |
| False-alarm handling | Share of unconfirmed signals recorded for correction, not action. |

## Physical Mission

| KPI | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Boundary compliance | Share of missions staying inside the defined boundary. |
| Pause-and-review rate | Share of exceptions paused and reviewed before adjustment. |
| Evidence completeness | Share of missions closed with required evidence. |
| Human-approval rate | Share of mission adjustments approved by an operator. |

## Integration Recovery

| KPI | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| In-flight preservation | Share of in-flight work preserved through the outage. |
| Resume verification | Share of resumed work with verified integration state. |
| Outage evidence | Completeness of outage-window and recovery evidence. |
| Escalation handling | Share of persistent outages escalated with evidence. |

## Engineering Assurance

| KPI | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Contract pass rate | Share of verification contracts passing before release. |
| Waiver discipline | Share of waivers with explicit human approval and evidence. |
| Re-verification coverage | Share of changes that triggered re-verification. |
| Attestation completeness | Share of releases with attestations recorded. |
