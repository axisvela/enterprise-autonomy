# Recruitment Requisition To Offer

## Business Scenario

A hiring manager requests a role. Talent acquisition validates the requisition, coordinates structured candidate evaluation, preserves decision evidence, and issues an offer only after accountable human approval. Automation supports work but does not make an unreviewed hiring decision.

| Item | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | Hiring manager submits a new or replacement requisition. |
| Accountable owner | Hiring manager and talent-acquisition lead. |
| Completion | Requisition is approved and an offer is issued, or it is rejected, paused, or cancelled with a reason. |
| Evidence | Requisition, role criteria, interview records, structured feedback, approvals, decision, and communication status. |

## Design Steps

1. Define business need, job-related criteria, interview stages, and accountable human decision makers.
2. Limit candidate-data access to personnel with hiring responsibility.
3. Establish structured, job-related evaluation criteria before interviews. Do not use protected characteristics or proxies as criteria.
4. Limit automation to administration, scheduling, retrieval, and evidence organization. Humans retain screening, selection, and offer decisions.
5. Define accommodation, privacy, complaint, retention, and escalation procedures under applicable law and policy.

## Operating Steps

1. **Validate requisition.** The manager submits business justification, role, location, type, budget reference, and criteria. Talent acquisition returns incomplete requests for correction.
2. **Approve need.** Authorized finance, workforce, and business owners approve or reject the requisition with recorded reasons.
3. **Prepare evaluation.** Recruiter and manager confirm criteria, panel, structured questions, and final decision owner before assessing candidates.
4. **Coordinate interactions.** Systems may assist scheduling and communications; recruiters remain accountable for their accuracy and appropriateness.
5. **Collect feedback.** Interviewers provide feedback against defined criteria. Missing, conflicting, or non-job-related feedback is returned for clarification, not converted into a recommendation.
6. **Select.** The accountable panel performs a human evidence review and records its decision rationale.
7. **Offer or close.** Authorized stakeholders approve the offer before the recruiter communicates it. Complaints, accommodation needs, fairness concerns, or data issues are escalated to the responsible human function.

## Public Boundary

This is a workflow illustration, not employment, legal, privacy, or compliance advice. It omits scoring, policy, retention, HR configuration, and decision models.
