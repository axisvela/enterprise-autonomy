# Public Business Scenarios

These scenarios illustrate outcome-level product composition. They are not deployment guides, contracts, or internal operating procedures.

| Scenario | Business Outcome | Primary Human Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Order fulfillment exception](../vertical-slices/order-fulfillment-exception.md) | Resolve an at-risk customer order with evidence and required approvals. | Order operations lead |
| [Procurement request to receipt](../vertical-slices/procurement-request-to-receipt.md) | Complete a controlled purchasing lifecycle. | Procurement owner |
| [Recruitment requisition to offer](../vertical-slices/recruitment-requisition-to-offer.md) | Run an accountable, human-governed hiring workflow. | Hiring manager and talent-acquisition lead |
| [Customer case resolution](../vertical-slices/customer-case-resolution.md) | Resolve a customer case across teams and systems. | Customer-service lead |
| [Predictive maintenance](../vertical-slices/predictive-maintenance-to-work-order.md) | Turn an equipment risk signal into a reviewed maintenance outcome. | Maintenance manager |
| [Mission to execution](../vertical-slices/mission-to-execution.md) | Execute a bounded physical inspection with human review. | Operations owner |
| [Simulation to validation](../vertical-slices/simulation-to-validation.md) | Review an autonomous operation before live use. | Designated review group |
| [Process to outcome](../vertical-slices/process-to-outcome.md) | Coordinate a long-running enterprise process. | Process owner |

Use the [public example design guide](example-design-guide.md) when adapting a scenario.

For product binaries, ports, databases, and interfaces, see the [technical catalog](technical-catalog.md).
